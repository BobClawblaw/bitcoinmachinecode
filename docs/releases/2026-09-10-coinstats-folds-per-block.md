# 2026-09-10 — The coinstats index folds per block during a bulk sync, like Core's

"Do what Core does with coinstats index and fold per block." Core's coinstatsindex folds every connected block's coin events into its MuHash and totals off the validation thread, from block 0, and writes a row per height. This node had two shapes: steady state folded per block through the fold worker, and bulk mode (a fresh sync, a reindex) left the index invalid and seeded it from one walk of the set at the caught-up downshift.

The deferral dated from 2026-09-06, when the fold ran on the connect thread (6.4 billion elements at 1.66 µs, about three hours of a fresh sync). The fold worker landed the same day and took the fold off the connect thread in steady state, and the deferral outlived its reason. What it cost: a fresh sync ended with no index until a walk of the whole set, no history rows for the synced range, and a history base rebuild with the offline builder afterwards. With #155 it would also have meant the index only existed after the download.

- **One shape in every mode**: boot adopts or seeds, the fold worker starts, the observers push coin records to the ring, the worker folds on its own core and writes the history row per block. The connect thread pays a memcpy a coin. `csi_defer_to_caught_up`, `csi_on_caught_up`, the engine's caught-up hook and the RPC's "unavailable during bulk catch-up" refusal are gone; the `csi_deferred` status field stays for the layout, always 0.
- **Cadence**: the commit marker follows the checkpoint, so during a bulk sync coinstats.dat lands every 1,024 blocks or 60 s (#155). The watermark rule is unchanged: the RPC waits for the worker to fold through the marker, and refuses rather than serve early.
- **Budget**: a heavy block is ~10k elements, 17 ms of worker time; the bench's apply at 25 blocks/s at 750,000 needs about 40% of one core in the worker. The ring holds 65,536 records and the producer waits up to 60 s for room before it overruns; an overrun invalidates the index (a lost record is a wrong digest, never a silent one), and boot re-seeds.

`test_coinstats_bulk_seed` rewritten: bulk mode with the worker and a shared status block, through the real connect path. The connect thread folds nothing, the worker folds all 200 elements, the loop exit's checkpoint pushes the marker, the watermark reaches the applied height, the file, the digest and the counters equal an independent walk, the history rows reach the applied height, and the downshift seeds no walk. The negative control (inline, steady) gives the same digest. The old test pinned the deferral (folds during bulk catch-up: 0) and would fail on this code by construction.

**To measure on the next fresh run:** the `[coinstats]` lines show the worker started at boot, no "seeded" line at the end, `gettxoutsetinfo` answering during the sync at the last checkpoint height, csi_lapped 0 in the heartbeat, and the history base complete without the builder.
