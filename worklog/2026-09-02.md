to run on. The unit burned its three restarts and went to failed, needing a
reset-failed. privatebroadcast is now commented out as well, which the FATAL
itself offers as the alternative. The privacy cost is real and is the point
worth stating plainly: our own transactions now leave over clearnet from this
node's address, and an observer can link them to this IP. That option should
only come back together with Tor or I2P.
