## Production: the B3 flush writer as a zombie (01:16Z)

- **What.** On `deploy-20261006d`, the async flush writer forked at block
  970,267 (pid 1959235, 01:16:41Z). It wrote `utxo_run_001417.dat` in
  milliseconds and then sat `<defunct>` for 22.5 min, until block 970,268's
  per-block poll reaped it.
- **Why.** `fz_poll` ran only per applied block, right after the fork, before
  the writer could have exited. At the tip that means one block interval.
- **Effect.** Safe: a crash replays the WAL, the next freeze waits, and
  shutdown reaps. Adopting the run, punching the WAL hole and releasing the
  frozen copy all lag by one block.
- **Fix.** Plan B3 reap: poll from the nothing-to-apply return and the
  worker's idle rotation. Landed in #400 (12:49Z). The test is #403
  (`tests/test_utxo_reap_at_tip`, watched to fail with either poll removed).

## Production: RPC dead 16:26Z to 18:43Z, out of file descriptors

- **What.** 1,023 of 1,024 descriptors open, accept() failing with EMFILE on
  RPC and the facade; mempool.space stalled at 970,365. P2P was unaffected.
- **Why.** The getblock reader lane (#383) kept a store handle (three
  descriptors) and an 8 MB buffer per thread and never released them. The
  facade runs a thread per connection: 314 `index.dat` handles were open.
- **Fix.** #402 (d0527354, merged as 54ffb790): a pthread-key destructor.
  `test_rpc_chain` went 14 → 206 descriptors unfixed, flat with the fix.
  Gate MAKE_EXIT=0.
- **Deploy.** `deploy-20261007a` at 18:42:56Z. RPC up at 18:43:46Z. Blocks
  970,378 and 970,379 hash-identical to the oracle, `NRestarts=0`, descriptors
  flat at 66–67. Still 66 at 20:42Z, at tip 970,389.
- Write-up: `docs/devlog/INCIDENT_2026-10-07_reader_lane_fd_leak.md`.
