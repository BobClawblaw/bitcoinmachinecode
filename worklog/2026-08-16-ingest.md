## BUG FOUND (live): duplicated non-contiguous tail on multi-seed continuation

The original `serve` ingest got to ~1390 heights clean, then across a
seed-switch boundary produced a duplicate tail: heights 1391..1609 repeated the
hashes of 0..218 (i.e. a genesis-anchored re-download appended as new heights).
`check_chain`: 219 duplicate records + 1 chain-break, tip 0..1609 "ARCHIVE
CORRUPT" where heights 0..1200 had been clean minutes earlier.

Root cause in `asm/daemon/main.c`:
- `anchor_locator()` anchored the peer locator to the stored tip via
  `node_serve_block()`. When that transiently returned <80 on a just-appended
  tip it silently fell back to an ALL-ZERO (genesis) locator. The next
  `node_sync` then re-requested from genesis and `store_append` (a blind
  sequential append, by design) appended those re-downloaded blocks at brand-new
  heights → duplicate, non-contiguous, corrupted archive.
- `outbound_catchup()` had the same inline genesis-collapse pattern.

Fix (commit ...):
- `anchor_locator()` now reads the stored tip's 32-byte hash straight from the
  index record (idx_len-48), which is always available and cannot misfire.
- `outbound_catchup()` reuses the fixed `anchor_locator()` (fwd-decl'd) instead
  of the inline node_serve_block/zero-collapse logic.

Verified:
- Same live sustained ingest, post-fix: clean to tip 3000+ (0 duplicate hashes,
  check_chain deep ARCHIVE CLEAN); the pre-fix corruption at ~1390 no longer
  occurs across multiple seed-switch points.
- Full `make test` green (0 failures) after the change.
