## M2: the heap (branch perf/2026-10-08-m2-memory)

The operator asked why bmc used twice Core's memory and how to stop using
70 GB. The breakdown of run 41's 75.6 GB peak, and what was built, are in
`worklog/2026-10-05-performance-holes-plan.md` (the 2026-10-08 section):

- The merge child and the flush writer are forked without the buffers they
  never read, so the parent's writes copy nothing for them. This was the
  32 GB heap peak.
- `dbcache` is the total: the live memtable and the frozen copy get half
  each.
- The frozen copy releases its pages when its writer is adopted.
