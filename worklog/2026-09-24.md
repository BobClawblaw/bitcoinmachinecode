- [x] **The window's tail was judged per CHUNK, not per block (0a11830e).**
      After the 13:56 restart the download sat at 823,243 for its last

- [x] **The 10-minute stop (ff623431).** 18:56:25Z the serve parent
      forwarded SIGTERM; the download worker's loop went silent (no [dlc]
      status line; utxo_live applied 24 blocks in ten minutes) and noticed
      the signal at 19:06:46Z, 11 s after the parent gave up at 610 s and
      exited. No EOWNERDEAD, so not the mempool lock. The worker's gettxout
      service point (`txoq_service`, "never blocks") answered with a
      blocking send on a socketpair that Darwin sizes at 8 KB each way

      a few rounds -- and shows that shutdown() of the parent's end does

      that stops reading gets no answers (gettxout refuses), the worker
      keeps connecting. **Fits the stop; not proven from the log** -- the
      14:34 stop of the old binary was clean (4 s), so the next slow stop

## Round 1 — the m5ultra bug report: the h=274443 halt, undo, addrindex, the dial storm

The BlockYard monitor's report (`~/bmc_osx_deploy/BUGS-2026-09-24.md`,
four items) against the first full production IBD. Fixed in order of
blast radius; the UTXO set rebuilt from the archive on the fixed sorter
(reindex-chainstate) with `bmc.bootcatchup=0`.

- [x] **The h=274443 FATAL was the sorter, not the chain (f9368f9e).**
      utxo_lsm_twin's radix variant C read the digit at
      `A[eidx * 64 + byteoff]` with a u32 eidx, which wraps at 2^26: a
      70,992,972-descriptor flush scrambled its tie groups, and the run's
      first-match scan reported a live coin absent. x86 zero-extends. Two
      latent x86-parity fixes in the same sorter. tests/sort_repro3.c
      simulates the flush at full scale (HEAD: 94 violations at 71M;
      fixed: 0 at 71M/100M/150M, ASan clean).
- [x] **The worker that ignored SIGTERM for 2.5 h (95041ee1).** Under the
      worker's SIGCHLD = SIG_IGN a blocking waitpid on a live child sleeps
      until every child exits; dlc_stop_workers blocked on its first
      SIGKILLed helper. Bounded WNOHANG reaps everywhere in the worker.
      tests/test_dlc_stop_sigign.
- [x] **The dial storm (06f0bc47, 63afd44c).** Darwin ignores SO_SNDTIMEO
      on connect(), so "Interrupted system call" was main.c's 20 s budget
      alarm: connect is now non-blocking + a 10 s poll. And on Darwin an
      SCM_RIGHTS socket arrives DEAD when the sender exits before the
      receiver's recvmsg; the dial helper now holds its end until the
      worker has it (212 of 261 leg closes were that). tests/test_dialhelper 1b.
- [x] **A halt froze nothing (b2f4b43c).** With live tracking switched off
      the public tip fell back to the STORED tip: getblockcount spoke for
      ~694k never-connected blocks and getblockstats on them failed "Can't
      read undo data" -- the report's item 1. A halted node now stays at
      its applied height. Same commit: getblockstats utxo_size_inc parity
      (PER_UTXO_OVERHEAD 41; the two BIP30 coinbases).
- [x] **addrindex disabled itself for a closable gap (5042f624).** A
      journal behind the history base is superseded, set aside as
      `addrindex.tail.stale`; the fresh-journal path adopts the base.
- [x] **Compaction wrote record by record (77fb3208):** the twin never
      ported mac_out_write/flush -- ~300M syscalls per full compaction;
      the boot's pre-catchup compaction ran 10+ minutes in write().
- [x] **getblockstats honours its stats filter (c5bf4a6f)**, Core 29.4's
      order, dedupe and errors.
