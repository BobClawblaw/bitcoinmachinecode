## What the code said before the log could

#348 (landed 23:50Z on 09-29 from the operator's other session, not yet
deployed) times the RPC execution lock so the next 90-second stall names
its holder. Asked how to stop the stalls rather than name them, the code
was read for anything with a 90-second signature: exactly two constants,
`SRT_WAIT_MS` and `SBK_WAIT_MS`, both the wait for the download worker's
acknowledgement over a shared-memory channel, both spun with the
execution lock held. The worker picks those channels up at the top of its
serve loop, and at a new block that loop is inside the block's passes,
each leg allowed 60 s. A `sendrawtransaction` (the Esplora facade's POST
/tx from mempool.space goes through it) landing in that window holds the
whole surface for up to 90 s. Production's log at the 09-29 22:54:21
stall shows two blocks applied in 0.04 s each and nothing else amiss --
the node was fine, the RPC process was waiting. A hypothesis until the
#348 line confirms it, but the only one in the code with that shape.

## Fix 1, prepared (PR #349, this branch)

Six places waited on the worker under the lock: `sbk_stage`
(submitblock / getblocktemplate proposals), `mpd_import_one`
(importmempool, per entry), `cmd_sendrawtransaction`, `tma_stage` and
`cmd_testmempoolaccept`, `cmd_submitpackage`, and `ctl_send` (addnode,
setban, disconnectnode). All now take the channel mutex through
`submit_lock()` / `submit_unlock()`, which release the execution lock
around the wait and take it back in the class it held
(`rpc_exec_yield_begin` / `_end` in `rpc_server.c`, weak-referenced so
the standalone rpcd and the unit tests link without the server). Two
things had to move with it: what a handler reads after the wait must be
its own (the reply buffers went from static to local, the error strings
returned through `*em` to thread-local), and the two package handlers
hold the mutex from their parse to their reply because their staging
arrays are static -- with the lock yielded, a second caller could
otherwise overwrite them between the parse and the staging.

Proof: `test_rpc_responsive` scenario E -- a status block nobody answers,
the wait cut to 2.5 s, every trivial method and an exclusive
`getblockhash` answering within 100 ms while a submit waits, the submit
timing out honestly at -4, the yield counter moved. Revert-checked: the
yield made a no-op, `getblockhash` waited 2,473 ms, the stall reproduced
in miniature. `-Werror` runtime, `bmc_rpcd`, link-check clean. The full
gate and the deploy wait for Core rerun #4 to finish (the box stays
quiet for a timed run); it lands with #348 as `deploy-20260929c`.

Not done, and named: the submitter itself still waits up to 90 s (fix 2,
the worker servicing the channel between legs); the read-only chain
queries still take the exclusive lock (fix 3, the lanes).
