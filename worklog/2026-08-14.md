      r8 but memcpy_len reads length from RDX -> copied [s_p] bytes, sweeping
      through .bss into stdout/stderr copies (0x143e6a0/0x143e6c0). Found with a
      hardware write watchpoint on the stdout slot (caught memcpy_len.c).
- [x] getheaders count from byte pointer + canonical CompactSize varint
      (0xFD+2 for >=253; compact single-byte otherwise).
