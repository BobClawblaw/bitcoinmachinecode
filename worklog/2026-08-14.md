## (start wallet bridge) pubkey decompression -- bitcoin_pubkey.asm [DONE]
fe_pow + pubkey_parse: recover affine Qx,Qy limbs from a compressed (02/03) or
uncompressed (04) secp256k1 pubkey, verified on G (0x02 even, 0x03 odd=p-Gy,
0x04), non-residue rejection, bad length, off-curve. This is the linchpin to
real tx validation: pubkey_parse(Qx,Qy) feeds the stand-alone verified
ecdsa_verify(z,r,s,Qx,Qy). Suite grew 34 -> 35.

DEBUG JOURNEY (notable):
- fe_pow square-and-multiply initially bit-indexed LSB-first (acc multiplied at
  bit0 on the first iteration) -- fixed to scan MSB-first (bit index == r15).
- CRITICAL NASM gotcha: `;` is a COMMENT EVEN MID-LINE. Every one-liner
  `instr1; instr2` silently dropped instr2+. Wrote /tmp/split_nasm.py to split
  all multi-instruction lines (the root cause of empty/zeros results).
- rsp parity: push/pop around calls inside a framed function shifts rsp by 8 and
  breaks the 8mod16 contract at the call (removed pushr8/push r15 around calls).
- Framesize: with 5 pushes, sub rsp must be 8 mod16 (0x198/0x1d8), not 0 mod16.
- x bytes are BIG-ENDIAN; fe_* takes LE limbs -- needed a bswap byte->limb load
  (the BE bytes were being fed straight in, giving wrong field values).
- Root "sqrt wrong" was a TRUNCATED constant: (p+1)/4 limb3 is 0x3FFFFFFFFFFFFFFF,
  I had 0x000000003FFFFFFF.
- test_harness bug: memcpy'd a 130-char hex STRING into u[65] -- ASAN caught it
  (not a pubkey bug).
