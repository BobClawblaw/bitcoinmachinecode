DEBUG JOURNEY (notable):

  bit0 on the first iteration) -- fixed to scan MSB-first (bit index == r15).
- CRITICAL NASM gotcha: `;` is a COMMENT EVEN MID-LINE. Every one-liner

### (final) end-to-end P2PKH spend validation [CAPSTONE DONE]
verify_p2pkh (bitcoin_script.asm): validate a P2PKH input spend end to end in
assembly -- scriptSig <sig><pubkey> on a raw tx, with the prevout scriptPubKey.
Steps (all verified vs an independent pure-Python self-validating signer):
  1. sighash_all(out32, tx, idx, prevout_script)  (legacy SIGHASH_ALL)
  2. walk raw tx to the input, parse its scriptSig's two pushes
  3. der_parse_sig(sig) -> r,s limbs + hashtype (require ==1)
  4. be_to_limbs(sighash) -> z ;  5. pubkey_parse(pub) -> Qx,Qy
  6. ecdsa_verify(z,r,s,Qx,Qy)
Valid spend -> 1; tampered sig -> 0. Both pass; 38/38 suite green.

DEBUGGING (3 distinct root causes, all documented):
- Test TX malformed: scriptSig field needs a CompactSize length prefix (0x6a for
  the 106-byte content) beyond the inner push opcodes; without it the wrong read
  let raw scriptSig bytes slip into the sighash preimage making it differ from
  the unsigned-tx sighash.
- Signature generator double-hashed (cryptography ECDSA(SHA256) hashes its
  input; Bitcoin signs the raw sighash). Switched to a pure-Python self-verified
  signer (self-check r==x) over the raw digest.
- STACK-LOCAL OVERLAP in verify_p2pkh: the hashtype slot sat inside the sighash
  32-byte span ([rbp-0x1f0..-0x1d1]); the 4-byte hashtype write to [rbp-0x1e0]
  clobbered the sighash, corrupting z/s in ecdsa_verify's args (seen via gdb).
  Moved htype to [rbp-0x68]; also kept rbx pristine (used r11 for stack-arg snap).
- der_parse_sig now normalizes 33-byte r/s with a leading 0x00 down to 32.
Closes the validation bridge: the node proves a real P2PKH spend's signature.
