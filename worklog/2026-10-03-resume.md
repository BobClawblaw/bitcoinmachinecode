## The 10-02 outage (23 h)

`deploy-20261002a` (1606aa36) applied 969,530 and was reported verified. It
segfaulted at 04:02Z, then crash-looped about 40 s after every restart (when
mempool.space re-synced through the facade), and systemd gave up at 04:28Z.
Nobody looked until the next session ran `systemctl status`.

- **Cause:** #368's `POST /internal/mempool/txs` called
  `rpc_chain_tx_blockhash` from the facade thread **without the execution
  lock**. `irs_refresh` rewrites `g_txi_runs` in place, zeroing each kept
  run's map while its counts stay set, so a racing lookup read through a NULL
  map.
- **How it was found without a core dump:** all 11 crashes faulted at the same
  address, `0x6a61e3bd8` = 48 + 1,425,612,630×20 (the run's `sparse_off`) +
  16×2,784,399 (the first binary-search midpoint).
- **Fix: #373.** The lookup now takes the lock, and `gettxspendingprevout`
  left the lock-free lane, which had the same race since 09-30.
- **Lessons** are in memory (`lock-free-callers-bypass-the-exec-lock`,
  `deploy-watch-the-next-block`): check `systemctl is-active` at the start of
  every session, and call a deploy verified only after 10+ minutes with
  NRestarts=0.
