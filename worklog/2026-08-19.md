- [x] Verified `legacy_sighash` against all 500 `sighash.json` vectors:
      `validation/gen_sighash_vectors.py` -> `tests/sighash_vec.h`,
      `tests/test_legacy_sighash.c`, **500/500**.

      `*hashtype` output is hardcoded to recognize only byte==1 (never
      updated from the SIGHASH_ALL-only era) -- switched to reading the
